# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance using Microsoft Excel. It organizes daily sales data into performance metrics and a dashboard to help compare sales executives, track target achievement, and identify performance gaps.
--
## Business Objective
The goal is to understand how sales executives are performing against a sales target and make it easier to identify high performers and executives who may need support.

--

## Tools and Skills
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas (`SUM` and percentage calculations)
- Data analysis and KPI reporting
- Dashboard charts and visual summaries
- Sales performance tracking

--

## Dataset
The workbook contains:
- **141 sales-executive records**
- Five daily sales fields (`Day1` to `Day5`)
- A target value for each executive
- Calculated total sales
- Target hit percentage
- Percentage away from target

The workbook includes a raw-data sheet and a dashboard sheet.

--

## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales:** Sum of sales across the five days for each executive.
- **Target Hit %:** Total sales divided by the target.
- **Away From Target %:** The portion of the target that remains unmet.
- **Top / Bottom Sales:** Compares executives by total sales.
- **Target Achievement Comparison:** Highlights how close each executive is to the target.

  --

## Sample Findings
Based on the raw data in this workbook:
- Records analyzed: **141**
- Combined sales across the five days: **38,945**
- Average five-day sales per executive: **276.2**
- Target per executive: **500**
- Records reaching the target: **0 of 141**

  --
  
## Business Value
This analysis can help a sales team monitor performance, compare results across executives, and prioritize coaching or follow-up where target achievement is low.

--

Business Recommendations
Based on the sales analysis, the following actions are recommended:
1.	Review sales targets: Check whether the target of 500 is realistic and appropriate for the period being analyzed.
2.	Identify performance gaps: Investigate why sales executives are falling short of the target.
3.	Provide training and support: Offer coaching to executives who need help improving their sales performance.
4.	Monitor daily sales: Track daily results regularly to identify problems early.
5.	Study top performers: Analyze the methods used by higher-performing executives and share effective practices with the team.
These recommendations can help improve sales monitoring and support better business decisions.
